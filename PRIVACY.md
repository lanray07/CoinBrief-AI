# CoinBrief AI Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 29, 2026

CoinBrief AI is an informational crypto and blockchain news research app. It does not provide trading, brokerage, custody, exchange, lending, staking, portfolio management, financial advice, price targets, or buy/sell/hold recommendations.

## Data Collection

The current App Store release of CoinBrief AI does not collect personal data from the app.

CoinBrief AI stores app preferences and saved in-app state locally on your device. This may include watchlist choices, notification preferences, saved articles, display settings, onboarding state, and subscription-related app state. This local information is used only to operate the app experience on your device.

## Subscriptions And Purchases

CoinBrief AI Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple through StoreKit and the App Store. Apple may process payment, subscription, and purchase information under Apple's own privacy policies. CoinBrief AI does not receive your full payment card details.

## Notifications

If you enable notifications, CoinBrief AI may schedule informational alerts on your device based on your selected preferences. Notification settings can be changed in the app or in iOS Settings.

## Analytics And Tracking

The current App Store release does not use third-party advertising, tracking, analytics SDKs, or advertising identifiers.

## External Links

The app or its App Store listing may link to external pages such as the public GitHub repository or support issue tracker. Information you provide to those external services is handled by the relevant service provider.

## Changes

If CoinBrief AI adds accounts, cloud sync, analytics, diagnostics, backend services, or other features that collect data, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before those features are submitted for review.
