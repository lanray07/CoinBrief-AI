# Icon Concept

Primary mark: a dark rounded square with a cyan editorial crosshair and a small amber source-check dot. The crosshair communicates focus and filtering; the dot communicates verified source evidence. Avoid coin logos, candles, trading arrows, dollar signs, and exchange-like motifs.

Palette:

- Background: midnight navy `#08111F`
- Primary mark: electric cyan `#12BFE8`
- Secondary accent: soft violet `#8B8DF8`
- Verification accent: amber `#F6B44B`

App icon production notes:

- Keep the mark readable at 29 pt.
- Do not use tiny text.
- Export all required sizes from a 1024 x 1024 master.
- Confirm the icon is distinct from exchanges, wallets, and market-data competitors.
