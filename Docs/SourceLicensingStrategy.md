# Source-Licensing Strategy

CoinBrief AI should launch with a rights-first source mix:

- Commercially licensed crypto/business news feeds for broad coverage.
- Direct publisher agreements for premium sources.
- Official primary sources such as protocol blogs, exchange announcements, regulator updates, company investor pages, and status pages.
- Public-domain or public-record materials where legally appropriate.
- User-facing attribution, publisher name, source link, timestamp, and source category on every story.

## Copyright Controls

- Summaries should be transformative, brief, and source-grounded.
- Avoid long excerpts and avoid reconstructing articles.
- Respect robots, feed terms, API terms, paywall restrictions, and syndication rules.
- Track source license, permitted use, retention window, and attribution requirements per source.

## Prelaunch Checklist

- Legal approval for each source class.
- Written provider contracts for commercial feeds.
- Retention policy for cached source text.
- Takedown workflow.
- Audit trail for every generated summary.
