# Accessibility Checklist

- Dynamic Type supported through semantic SwiftUI fonts.
- VoiceOver labels on story cards, verification badges, watchlist rows, and paywall actions.
- Sentiment and status never depend on red/green color alone.
- Tap targets meet minimum comfortable sizes.
- Reduce Motion respected by avoiding required animation-only cues.
- Color contrast reviewed in light and dark mode.
- Important alerts are text-described and icon-described.
- Audio briefing has a transcript and source list.
- Widgets use concise, readable text at all widget sizes.
- Onboarding and paywall can be completed with VoiceOver.
