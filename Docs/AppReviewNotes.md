# App Review Notes

CoinBrief AI is a standalone, independently developed source-controlled research reader for crypto protocols, security updates and digital-asset regulation. It does not enable cryptocurrency exchange, custody, brokerage, trading, staking, lending, investment management, financial advice, price targets, or buy/sell/hold recommendations.

Users build a research desk from live RSS and Atom feeds, inspect the evidence record for each report, search across enabled sources, save reports locally, and reopen the publisher's original page. Pro unlocks custom feed URLs and Deep Dive mode.

## Reviewer Access

- The release build uses `LiveNewsService`; it does not display bundled demo stories.
- Built-in live feeds are published by the Ethereum Foundation, U.S. SEC, U.S. CFTC, and Bitcoin Optech.
- The Sources tab lets reviewers enable or disable each feed. A network connection is required for the first refresh; the last successful reports are cached locally afterward.
- No sign-in is required for the reviewable free experience.
- Paid features use StoreKit 2 product IDs:
  - `com.coinbriefai.pro.monthly`
  - `com.coinbriefai.pro.annual`
- Restore purchases and manage subscription controls are available in Profile.

## Review Path

1. Launch the app and complete the short evidence-focused onboarding.
2. Open Sources to inspect the enabled feed library.
3. Return to Briefing and pull to refresh.
4. Open any report and review its Evidence record and Verify sections.
5. Tap the bookmark button, then open Saved to confirm the excerpt and original source link remain available.
6. Open Profile > CoinBrief AI Pro to test custom-source and Deep Dive entitlement activation.

## Safety Language

The app repeatedly labels itself as research only. It is not financial advice and should not be used as the sole basis for investment decisions.
