# App Review Notes

CoinBrief AI is an informational crypto and blockchain news research app. It does not enable cryptocurrency exchange, custody, brokerage, trading, staking, lending, investment management, financial advice, price targets, or buy/sell/hold recommendations.

Users can read source-backed summaries, follow topics, save articles, configure informational alerts, and optionally subscribe to Pro for more summaries, deeper explainers, audio briefings, offline access, filters, and widgets.

## Reviewer Access

- The app includes mock/demo data in development builds.
- No sign-in is required for the reviewable free experience.
- Paid features use StoreKit 2 product IDs:
  - `com.coinbriefai.pro.monthly`
  - `com.coinbriefai.pro.annual`
- Restore purchases and manage subscription controls are available in Profile.

## Safety Language

The app repeatedly labels itself as news and research only. It is not financial advice and should not be used as the sole basis for investment decisions.
