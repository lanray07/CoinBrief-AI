1. **What the app does and the problem it solves**

CoinBrief AI is a source-controlled research reader for crypto protocol, security, and regulatory information. Users manage a library of RSS and Atom sources, refresh a live briefing, search and filter the resulting reports, inspect an evidence record for each item, save reports locally, and reopen the publisher's original page. Each evidence record retains the publisher, publication time, source type, excerpt, and original URL. The app solves the problem of losing provenance when information from many crypto sources is condensed into a generic headline feed. It does not execute transactions, track a portfolio, predict prices, or recommend trades.

2. **Intended users**

The intended users are technically curious crypto participants, protocol developers, compliance and policy researchers, security professionals, and readers who follow primary-source updates but do not want trading signals. They need to review protocol announcements, public regulatory records, technical research, and security disclosures while retaining a direct path back to the evidence.

3. **Market gap**

Many crypto apps focus on prices, portfolios, trading, or a publisher-controlled news stream. CoinBrief AI instead gives the user control over the source library and makes provenance part of the reading workflow. Users can enable or disable built-in first-party and public-record feeds, add their own RSS or Atom feeds with Pro, see automatic non-investment topic/risk classifications, inspect the evidence fields beside every brief, and retain the original source link in saved research. The distinguishing value is a user-controlled evidence desk, not another price tracker or anonymous AI-news feed.
