# Privacy Policy Draft

Effective date: To be finalized before launch.

CoinBrief AI provides source-backed crypto and blockchain news summaries. This draft describes the intended privacy posture for the first production version. It must be reviewed against the final app, backend, analytics, and third-party SDK behavior before App Store submission.

## Data We Expect To Collect

- Watchlist topics and saved-story preferences, if sync is enabled.
- App settings such as summary mode, notification preferences, quiet hours, and display preferences.
- Subscription entitlement status through Apple StoreKit.
- Basic diagnostics needed to keep the app reliable.

## Data We Do Not Intend To Collect

- Brokerage credentials.
- Wallet private keys or seed phrases.
- Trading account data.
- Precise location.
- Contact lists.
- Advertising identifiers.

## How Data Is Used

Data is used to personalize news briefings, deliver requested notifications, sync saved items, manage subscriptions, prevent abuse, and improve app reliability.

## Third Parties

Production releases may use licensed news providers, AI infrastructure, authentication, cloud storage, push notification systems, and diagnostics. Their data practices must be included in App Store Connect privacy answers before submission.
