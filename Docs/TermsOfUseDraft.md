# Terms Of Use Draft

CoinBrief AI is an informational news and research product. It does not provide financial, investment, legal, tax, accounting, trading, brokerage, custody, exchange, staking, or lending services.

## User Responsibility

Cryptoassets are volatile and risky. Users are responsible for their own research and decisions. CoinBrief AI summaries may be incomplete, delayed, incorrect, or based on sources that later change.

## AI Summaries

AI-generated summaries are created from attributed sources. The app should not show a summary when source evidence is unavailable, but users should still review original sources before relying on any information.

## Subscriptions

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. Users can manage or cancel subscriptions through their Apple account settings. A free tier remains available with limited functionality.

## Content Rights

News content, names, marks, and source materials remain the property of their respective owners. CoinBrief AI provides attribution and links to original sources.

## No Investment Advice

Nothing in CoinBrief AI is a recommendation to buy, sell, hold, or trade any asset.
